Page 02, the number: how the page arrives

Frames are the real page frozen at four moments after a tap; the bars below are the choreography, all CSS transforms, opacity and clip-path.

160 msInk roll

The coral field wipes in from the right edge over the cover (clip-path, 420 ms, ease-ink). Content waits for the field.

600 msOdometer

Digits roll up to 1,307, right column first (900 ms, ease-data, 45 ms stagger). The commas never move.

1,000 msStamp and split

The stamp lands on a spring (about 240 ms); the series bar grows ahead of the hatched films bar; milestones pop in.

1,400 msSettled

Everything is still within 1.4 s of the tap. A tap during the choreography completes it; reduced motion shows this frame after a 120 ms crossfade.

Rules

  • One lead movement per page, then support in reading order.
  • Only transform, opacity, clip-path and one font axis animate; nothing loops.
  • Low-end phones swap the width stretch for scaleX and drop the grain blend.
  • Reduced motion: final state, 120 ms crossfade, autoplay off.
  • Progress is a CSS scaleX keyframe: no React render per frame.

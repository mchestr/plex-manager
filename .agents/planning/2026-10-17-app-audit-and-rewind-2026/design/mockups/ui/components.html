Rewind scope contract

Rewind 2026 is designed separately. This is what it may change, what it inherits, and what it adds. The token-contrast test runs on every allowed field.

Viewer controls are the app's Buttons under the scope
ReadyChapter 3 of 14

Rewind must define

background, foreground, card, card-foreground, popover, popover-foreground, primary, primary-hover, primary-foreground, secondary, secondary-hover, secondary-foreground, muted, muted-foreground, accent, accent-foreground, border, border-strong, input, ring, chart-1..5

Inherited unchanged

success, warning, info, destructive (re-declared at their dark values), font-sans, font-mono, radius scale, spacing, focus rule, control sizes, z-index scale, reduced-motion rules

Rewind adds

--rw-field, --rw-ink, --rw-spot (per member or scene), --font-display, --rw-* motion and texture tokens. Fields: dark L 0.22 to 0.34 with chroma 0.03 or more; primary stays in the amber family.

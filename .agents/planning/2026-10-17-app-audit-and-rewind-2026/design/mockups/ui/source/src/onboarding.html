Get the best picture

Many Plex apps cap video at 720p. The server then converts it as you watch, which softens the picture and can cause buffering.

How to fix it

  1. Open Settings in the Plex app on your device.
  2. Go to Quality. On some TVs it's under Video.
  3. Set Remote streaming to Maximum or Original.
  4. Turn off Adjust automatically.
{{i:info}}
Each device has its own setting. Repeat this on every TV, phone and browser you watch on.

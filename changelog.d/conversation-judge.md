### Added

- **Conversation judge:** a second model can review admin assistant conversations and record what the assistant got wrong: a wrong or unsupported fact, a wrong or missing tool, an unneeded refusal or a misread question. Admins start a review from a conversation's page, or turn on a nightly review of changed conversations (off by default). Each finding points at its message and can be marked fixed, won't fix or a false alarm. The judge has its own model and Daily budget, sends nothing while AI features are off, and never reviews members' Discord chats. Credentials are redacted from everything it reads. So the judge can check answers, the assistant now stores each answer's tool calls with the conversation, redacted and encrypted, and they go when the conversation does.

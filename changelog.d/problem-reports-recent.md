### Added

- **Report a problem from what you watched:** `/report` now lists what the member is watching now and what they watched recently, so most reports need no search. For a playback problem it asks whether anything else plays on the member's last player, and for a soft picture after a converted play it points to the playback guide first. Each report records the member's last play of the title and whether it has played to the end since its file was added, for the server admin.

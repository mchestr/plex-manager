### Fixed

- **Inactive members and a play in progress:** the daily last seen read now also reads the plays open in Tautulli at that moment. A member who is still watching, or left a play paused, counts as seen at the time of the read. Before, Tautulli counted a play only after it stopped, so a member who started a film late in the evening could lose access in the middle of it at the daily run. When Tautulli cannot list its open plays, the run stores the recorded plays but removes no one, and the run list on the Inactive members page says why.

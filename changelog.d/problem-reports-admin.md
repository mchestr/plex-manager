### Added

- **Problem reports for the server admin:** Automations has a new first tab, Problem reports, that lists reports by Needs you, Fixing, Fixed and Closed. A report's page shows the timeline, each member's words and what Tautulli said, and offers Mark fixed and Close with a note, each with a note the member who reported it sees on `/report` and an internal note. The Automations badge counts the reports waiting for you, and the feature's switch is at the bottom of the tab.
- **Discord alerts for new reports:** when the bot is on, admins who linked Discord get a DM for each new report, with at most one message an hour about any one member's reports.
- **Swaps need approval:** a switch on a member's admin page that will keep automatic swaps of the copy for their reports waiting for you.

### Changed

- **Problem reports off:** with the switch off, members who made or follow a report still see and can cancel it on `/report`.
- **Automations** now opens on Problem reports instead of Watchlist sync.
